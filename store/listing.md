## Long description
Still Sudoku is Sudoku without the noise. No ads, no accounts, no timers screaming at you. Just the puzzle.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Four difficulty levels (Easy to Expert) graded by the solving techniques each puzzle actually needs, and every puzzle has exactly one solution
• Pencil notes with automatic note cleanup, plus fill-notes when you want a head start
• Unlimited undo and redo
• Tap a cell then a number, or pick a number then tap cells
• Daily puzzle that is the same for everyone, with a calendar of the days you've finished

LEARN AS YOU GO
• Three-step hints that teach: first where to look, then why, then the move
• Technique names and explanations, from naked singles to X-Wing and beyond
• Check your work any time or turn on gentle mistake highlighting; there is no game over

MADE TO BE CALM
• Six themes with light and dark modes
• Large-text, colorblind-friendly and reduced-motion options
• Optional haptics and soft sounds
• Works fully offline

YOURS, ONLY YOURS
• No sign-up, no tracking, no data collected. Your games and statistics stay on your device
• Personal statistics by difficulty, with trends and best times

Still Sudoku is a small, independent app. If it helps you unwind, a rating means a lot.
