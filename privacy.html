Privacy Policy

Still Sudoku · Last updated: 2026-10-28

Still Sudoku is a puzzle game. It is built to need nothing from you except a few minutes of your time. In short: we do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

What is stored on your device

To let you continue a puzzle and keep your statistics, the app saves the following only on your own device (in the browser’s local storage or the app’s private storage): your current and past puzzles, your game statistics and scores, and your settings such as theme and input preferences. This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and we do not have a server to receive it.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data of the app in your browser, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling removes it permanently.

Network access

The app works fully offline. When used as a website, your browser downloads the app’s files from the host that serves it, and that host may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address and time of request) under its own policy. The installed iOS and Android apps do not contact any server.

Children

The app contains no ads, no purchases, no links out, and collects no information, so it is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example if optional cloud sync is added in the future, the updated policy will be posted here with a new date, and the change will be described in the app’s release notes before any data handling begins.
